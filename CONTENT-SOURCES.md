The new site keeps the exported Figma compositions and artwork in this repository. Event information, approved organiser profiles, portraits, wordmarks and the campus map were migrated from the previous `5anjay-s/aws-scd` website. The organiser supplied the event dates, registrations and community channel figures.

The past speakers belong to previous community events. They are not advertised as the SCD 2026 lineup. KonfHub is the official main-day ticket destination; IMPULSE registration is separate on Luma.

The attendee badge templates use the existing Figma artwork. Photos and names are processed only in the browser.

Bengaluru Tech Week wordmark: official horizontal SVG from https://bengalurutechweek.com/2026/media-kit. Artwork is used as supplied, scaled proportionally with clearspace on a light background.

Campus map: terrain.app, © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
